Open surgery

Laparoscopic & robotic surgery

Introducing Revelascope

A surgical retractor with an integrated camera and lighting, designed to bring the operative field to a shared monitor.

Under development by Crescata Surgical
First application / Breast surgery

A wide dissection.
A small incision.

Revelascope is being developed for nipple-sparing mastectomy, where visualization can become difficult beyond the incision.

The challenge of direct line of sight

A limited opening can make it difficult to see a deep or narrow operative field.

Visualization within an open workflow

An integrated camera and illumination bring a video view to the retractor itself.

A view the team can share

A shared monitor is central to the Revelascope concept.

The Revelascope difference

A different approach to
surgical visualization

Revelascope combines a shared video view with the instruments
and tactile feedback of open surgery.
